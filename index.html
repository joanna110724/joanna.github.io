I hope to study how neural circuits compute, store information, and learn through time. My research interests lie at the intersection of dynamical systems, spiking neural networks (SNNs), and synaptic plasticity.

During my undergraduate years, computational neuroscience and programming were most exciting, despite my biology-heavy courses. I also had opportunities to work with human embryonic tissue and stem cells. This inspired me to go into research, and I hope to continue learning more about the circuits in our brain.

Research Interests

Biophysical Network Dynamics

Investigating balanced states (E/I ratio), oscillatory rhythms, and low-dimensional manifolds in cortical microcircuits.

Local Synaptic Plasticity

Formulating spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP) and three-factor learning rules that enable biological credit assignment.

Surrogate Gradient Learning

Training recurrent spiking neural networks on temporal tasks using differentiable surrogate derivatives in PyTorch.

Neural Data Analysis

Working with high-density electrophysiology (Neuropixels) pipelines, spike sorting, and population dimensionality reduction.
